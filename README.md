# FujiCam 📷

A minimal camera app that applies Fujifilm film recipes to your photos in real-time.

## Features

- 📸 Live camera viewfinder with film simulation preview
- 🎞️ 6 authentic Fujifilm film recipes:
  - Classic Cuban Neg
  - Classic Chrome
  - Velvia
  - Provia
  - Astia
  - ACROS
- 💾 Save processed photos directly to your device
- 🔄 Retake photos with different recipes
